# Retired pages

The old landing pages (bootcamps, emergency, Bridgerton, Ruby, notices and friends) and the components only they used, kept as they were before the Oct 2026 logbook redesign. Nothing in this folder is built or linted. Their content still lives in `src/content/landing/`.

To bring a page back, move it (and any component it needs) back under `src/` and fix its imports.
