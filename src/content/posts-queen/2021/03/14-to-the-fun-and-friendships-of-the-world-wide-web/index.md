Hello there,

It’s been 25+ years since I first went online. It was an underwhelming experience. But also one that hinted at something exciting. Because I remember it so clearly. The sound of the modem. The thrill of typing [db.no](http://db.no/) (or was is [vg.no](http://vg.no/)) into the address bar. Mom making herself a coffee while we waited for something to happen. The page loading slowly, the regret of clicking a link as the whole process started again.

Fast forward 3-4 years, and I was keeping in touch with Jonatan via ICQ. Guri and myself trading desktop wallpaper collages we made with images found online. What did we put on those wallpapers, Guri? I am guessing mine were all Leonardo DiCaprio and Jared Leto.

It was **fun**, it was **informal**, it was **creative**, it was all about **friendships**…

And for me, this weird year has brought that all back.

In between the front-end framework wars and shitposting on Twitter, I found friendships and fun:

- Eva ([includeJS.dev](https://includejs.dev/)) and the ladies of her JavaScript Study group
- The Natterpoppers, (not so) secret gangle of Twitter dev buddies
- Monica and her [Blogging for Devs Community](https://bloggingfordevs.com/)
- [Clare and Christer](https://twitter.com/SnutiHQ) and their [SnutiLand Discord](https://t.co/bKosbLYSxi?amp=1) community

Thank you all for being there/here 💕

Until next week,\
