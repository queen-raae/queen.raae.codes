Hello there,

Signal has soared in popularity lately!

Why? The New York Times tells the story in ["Millions Flock to Telegram and Signal as Fears Grow Over Big Tech."](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/01/13/technology/telegram-signal-apps-big-tech.html)

Maybe you are one of its new users?\
Or perhaps you were convinced way back when I recommended it a year ago?

Either way, here is my favorite Signal feature you might not know about:

It is possible to message yourself, and by doing so, get a personal and encrypted notebook. Great if you would like to note something down before a doctor's appointment, for instance.

You may even do it as a voice memo!

And if you install Signal on your computer, you get instant access to your encrypted personal notes there as well.

Are you onboard the Signal train?
