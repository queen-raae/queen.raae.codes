Carissa Véliz on [Computer Says Maybe](https://open.spotify.com/episode/56ufF9ZV1abG8NEc1SYsE5): when tech leaders predict what AI will do to us, it's not a forecast, it's a directive. The more of us act on it, the truer it gets.

The way I hear it, the predictions are marketing. "Everything changes, get on board or get left behind" sells better than any feature list.
