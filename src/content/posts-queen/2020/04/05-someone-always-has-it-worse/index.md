I titled this email, *Someone always has it worse*, as I hear myself and others dismiss our frustrations with the new reality by reminding ourselves that there are people who have it so much worse.

As always, I felt conflicted by this practice. Yes, there will always be people who have it worse! But on the other side, your reality is your reality!

Then I listened to [Brené on Comparative Suffering, the 50/50 Myth, and Settling the Ball](https://pca.st/ak0w63qu), and she explains it all so well.

I will not try to summarise, I am still just an engineer after all, but I found it helpful and I hope you do too.

If you are not familiar with Brené Brown, she is a researcher who has spent the past two decades studying courage, vulnerability, shame, and **empathy**.
