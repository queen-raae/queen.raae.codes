Hello,

I’ll start with the book recommendation and get to the open-source part.

They feel connected to me, but I also just want everyone to read this book!

The book is [Invisible Women: Data Bias in a World Designed for Men](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/41104077-invisible-women) by Caroline Criado Perez.

Imagine a world where your phone is too big for your hand, where your doctor prescribes a drug that is wrong for your body, where in a car accident you are 47% more likely to be seriously injured, where every week the countless hours of work you do are not recognised or valued. **If any of this sounds familiar, chances are that you’re a woman**.Yes, it does sound familiar, and trying to find research-backed information about menstrual cycles while making POW! I have again felt it first hand…

I never thought I’d dream of a unicorn startup. Still, a tiny part of me kind of hopes POW! become one now. So I can personally fund research into female health generally and the menstrual cycle specifically.

However, I think realistically, this is not on the table. But my contribution could be a much smaller one. One I am confident I could get off the ground: **An open-source menstrual cycle analytics engine.**

I have not decided yet if this is the correct path forward for me, for POW!

Should I?\
Would you like to join me?\
Anything else?
