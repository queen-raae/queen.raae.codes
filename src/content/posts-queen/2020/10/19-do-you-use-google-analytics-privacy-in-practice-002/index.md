Hello,

Do you add Google Analytics by default to all projects?

I used to, but maybe "give that another think?" as I so eloquently put it in the latest [Privacy in Practice video](https://youtu.be/u6Wkw7Wj-3Y).

[![](./image-1.png)](https://youtu.be/u6Wkw7Wj-3Y)

These days I use [Fathom](https://usefathom.com/), but I also hear good things about [Simple Analytics](https://simpleanalytics.com/) and [Plausible](https://plausible.io/).

If you enjoy spinning up servers and hosting things yourself, [Umami](https://umami.is/) might be the solution for you.

Have a great week,\
Benedicte
