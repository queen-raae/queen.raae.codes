Hello again,

This week I want to give a huge shout-out to the Norwegian Consumer Council (Forbrukerrådet) for standing up for privacy!

[Out of Control](https://www.forbrukerradet.no/out-of-control/) is their newest campaign, shedding light on how the adtech industry exploits consumers.

Their report reveals, amongst other things, that popular dating apps, such as Grindr, share location, sexual preferences, and more with third parties.

They are also filing a formal complaint against Grindr, directed to the Norwegian Data Protection Agency.

It is great to see them taking a stand and upholding Norway's long-standing strict views on privacy.

Maybe privacy is our new oil?
