**Why you need a password manager?**

The experts agree you need strong and unique passwords.

By strong, they mean somewhere above 20 characters, and by unuiqe, they mean never use the same password for multiple services.

If you adhere to these rules, I am pretty sure you will inevitably forget a password or two!

And this is where a password manager comes in. You keep all your passwords in it, and whenever you log in somewhere, you retrieve it from the password manager.

It takes some getting used to, and the initial setup is a little cumbersome. But believe you me, it is worth it.

I recommend [1Password](https://1password.com/). I have been using it for three years. I really like their apps in addition to their brand as a whole.

If you feel like a password manager is too much for you, get a notebook and write down your password. It's actually not as stupid as it might seem, [check out this article by security expert Troy Hunt](https://www.troyhunt.com/password-managers-dont-have-to-be-perfect-they-just-have-to-be-better-than-not-having-one/).
