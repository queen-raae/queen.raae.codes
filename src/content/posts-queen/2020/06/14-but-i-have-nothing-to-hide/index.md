Of course, you don't.

But are you sure that will always be true?

With the latest developments in the world, I feel like thinking about our privacy is even more critical than ever.

We never know when it's our time to protest. Or when we might fall on the outside of what is considered "good" by the authorities.

My thoughts on this are not fully formed yet, but I found these two TED talks useful inputs in the process of learning more about privacy as a concept:

- [What you need to know about face surveillance](https://www.ted.com/talks/kade_crockford_what_you_need_to_know_about_face_surveillance) by Kade Crockford
- [Why privacy matters](https://www.ted.com/talks/glenn_greenwald_why_privacy_matters) by Glenn Greenwald
